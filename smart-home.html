CONNECTED HOME, THOUGHTFULLY PLANNED

More than a smart
light switch.

Smart-home projects can combine multiple systems around the way people use each space. Availability and compatibility depend on the selected products and site assessment.

✳
01 / AMBIENCE

Smart lighting

App-controlled lights, dimming, tunable white and preset scenes for work, relaxation and entertainment.

  • On/off and dimming
  • Warm-to-cool white
  • Room and zone scenes
▦
02 / CONTROL

Smart switches & panels

Connected wall controls and smart modules for convenient management of selected lights and appliances.

  • Touch or app control
  • Schedules and timers
  • Centralised control options
⌁
03 / RESPONSE

Sensors & automation

Motion, occupancy and ambient-light sensing can trigger actions based on activity and surrounding conditions.

  • Motion-based lighting
  • Occupancy response
  • Daylight-aware control
⌂
04 / COMFORT

Appliance & climate control

Explore compatible control for selected fans, air conditioners, plugs and everyday electrical appliances.

  • Selected appliance control
  • Fan and AC integration
  • Schedules and routines
◇
05 / PEACE OF MIND

Security & safety

Depending on the system, connected cameras, door sensors, smart locks and safety sensors can add awareness.

  • Door and window sensors
  • Camera and access options
  • Smoke / leak sensor options
▤
06 / DAYLIGHT

Curtain automation

Motorised curtains and blinds can be scheduled or included in scenes to coordinate privacy and natural light.

  • Open / close control
  • Timed routines
  • Scene integration
